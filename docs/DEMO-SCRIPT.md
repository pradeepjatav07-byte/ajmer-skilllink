# Product demo script (5–7 minutes)

## 1. The problem (30 seconds)

“In Ajmer, people often rely on a saved phone contact or a WhatsApp group when something breaks. Customers cannot easily compare trust, availability or price expectations, while capable local professionals have no consistent digital profile. Ajmer SkillLink makes the first connection clearer.”

## 2. Customer discovery (1½ minutes)

- Open the Customer portal overview.
- Search “AC repair” or choose Electrical / Plumbing.
- Show neighbourhood and verification filters.
- Open a Skill Passport. Point out skill badges, completed jobs, review summary, response time, service area and transparent Trust Score factors.
- Add two local professionals to Compare and show estimated ranges.

## 3. FairQuote and safe booking (1½ minutes)

- Open a provider and call out the FairQuote base range, possible visit fee, emergency surcharge and final-price disclaimer.
- Start a booking. Set a fictional Ajmer area/address, visit time, short scope and safety preferences.
- Use the safe-share action; show that the summary omits the exact address and contact information.
- For Emergency Mode, explain that only available, emergency-ready demo providers are shown and dangerous situations need appropriate emergency services.

## 4. Professional workflow (1 minute)

- Use the profile menu to switch to the Professional portal in offline demo mode, or sign in with `provider@ajmerskilllink.demo` in API mode.
- Open a new request, accept it, then progress to “Provider on the way”, “Work started” and “Work completed”.
- Show that each step appears in the customer timeline. Mention that work-proof is metadata-only in this version and earnings are illustrative demo accounting.

## 5. Customer completion and feedback (45 seconds)

- Return to the Customer portal. Confirm the completed work and submit a rating.
- Demonstrate the booking-history timeline and mention that a support ticket can be linked to a booking.

## 6. Admin trust operations (1 minute)

- Switch to the Admin portal.
- Review KPI cards, service-demand and local area activity.
- Approve a fictional provider from the verification queue. Show the audit trail.
- Open FairQuote rules or Complaints & disputes and export CSV.

## Suggested short pitch

**Problem:** Ajmer customers lack a trustworthy, comparable way to find skilled local help; independent professionals lack a reliable digital identity and customer funnel.

**Solution:** Ajmer SkillLink combines local discovery, professional Skill Passports, transparent FairQuote ranges, booking/status tracking, safe sharing, work-proof metadata and admin moderation.

**Differentiators:** Trust Score factors are explained rather than hidden; FairQuote separates estimate, visit fee and emergency surcharge; Emergency Mode focuses on availability and safe expectations; customer/provider/admin flows are distinct.

**Future scope:** production verification partnerships, consent-based secure media storage, richer scheduling and area pricing, multilingual support, notifications, dispute operations, and only later—carefully scoped payment and location integrations.

## Notes for presenters

- Demo data is fictional. Do not describe profile checks as government certification.
- Do not imply that Emergency Mode is a real emergency-response network.
- Prices are indicative, not a guaranteed quote.
- Do not describe browser localStorage as MySQL persistence; use the API-connected mode for the backend walkthrough.
